# 2026-10-31 · Plans/Todos redo & secondary-menu fix log

Redid the Plans module: split Plans and Todos into two separate pages and linked them (a todo can belong to a plan and counts toward its completion). Also redesigned the whole preview.

Two bugs were hit and fixed along the way:

(1) The secondary menu (the FAB popup) text spilled out of its box — root cause was a legacy `.fab button` descendant selector crushing the chip buttons into 56px circles; after changing it to `.fab>button` (targeting only the main button), the chips size to their text and become right-aligned, inward-extending translucent glass pills.

(2) All animations vanished after the first pass — that was a dirty `animation:none` style injected by the editor; once removed, the orb's float, click press-back, and floating dock all came back. Verified by smoke test 66/66, NUL sentinels intact.
